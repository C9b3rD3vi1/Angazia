📰 About Angazia

Kenya's premier tech talent marketplace — connecting skilled developers with world-class companies.

// OUR MISSION

Empowering Kenya's Tech Talent to Thrive

Angazia was born from a simple realisation: Kenya produces world-class tech talent, yet too many skilled developers struggle to find the right opportunities. We set out to change that.

Our platform bridges the gap between exceptional tech professionals and the companies that need them. We combine smart matching, transparent salary data, and a deep understanding of Kenya's tech ecosystem to make hiring fair, fast, and effective.

Every day, we help developers across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, and beyond find roles where they can do their best work. And we help companies build the teams that will define Kenya's digital future.
